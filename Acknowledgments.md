## 🙏 Acknowledgments & Open-Source Credits

StimTake Studio stands on the work of developers who shared their knowledge and code with the community.

Special thanks to:

- [MountainGod2](https://github.com/MountainGod2)
- [actions-user](https://github.com/actions-user)
- The contributors behind the `chaturbate_poller` project

Their work on `chaturbate_poller` provided an important reference and foundation while developing StimTake Studio's Chaturbate event-detection and polling workflow.

StimTake Studio expanded that foundation into a larger Windows creator toolkit including:

- Chaturbate tip detection
- StimTakeChaturbateBridge
- Chrome integration
- Local StimTake Studio runtime
- OBS Browser Source overlays
- Supporter tracking
- Action Deck automation
- Customizable tip-triggered actions
- Daily and Halloween Show Packs
- Animated interactive games and overlays

Open-source development moves forward because people are willing to share their work and knowledge. Thank you to MountainGod2, actions-user, and the `chaturbate_poller` contributors for making their work available and providing part of the foundation that StimTake Studio was able to build upon.
